# Surface: CV page (/about)

Mode: Persuade. A recruiter skims fit and seniority, then reaches out. Must carry: personality and craft, and the physics-to-code story. Wrong if: template-like, corporate, or dark hacker look. Nothing visual is sacred; content and function stay (drill-down per assignment, contact).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The CV as a depot destination blind: a roll of bias-cut cloth courses stepping under one fixed window. It rejects the sidebar-and-timeline résumé.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched cobalt duck cloth, amber condensed caps legends, calico panels carry all prose; oxblood only on the open course. One family (Sofia Sans, Extra Condensed for legends). A punched eyelet tape is the index. Square panels with a 12° bias on legend strips, stitched hairlines, and a visible weave.

STORY: Physicist turned .NET/full-stack developer. Every assignment is a destination on the roll: client, period, role. The visitor opens the ones that matter, then emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Cobalt field. VAN BROECK DIETER in huge amber caps fills the window; the next course, SOFTWARE DEVELOPER, is half-visible under the seam. A calico lead panel on the right holds the matted photo, the physics line and the email. The eyelet index runs down the left edge.

FORM: Destination blind (dealt challenger operate-c-depot-blind-courses, bolder re-roll 1); seed key be555059. Code-led. Signature interaction: a sticky window ribbon names the current course and snaps one course with an overshoot as you scroll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
